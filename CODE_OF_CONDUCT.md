# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

AgentWisper welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience
levels. Project spaces should remain professional, constructive, and focused on
the work.

Expected behavior includes:

- giving specific, respectful technical feedback;
- assuming good intent while discussing evidence;
- respecting privacy and avoiding disclosure of personal information;
- accepting maintainers' scope, safety, and release decisions;
- correcting mistakes without personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
content, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing another person's private
information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this
standard is violated.

To report conduct privately, follow the private-contact process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and state that the report concerns community conduct.
Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

## Scope

This policy applies to project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests,
reviews, discussions, and public representation of the project.
